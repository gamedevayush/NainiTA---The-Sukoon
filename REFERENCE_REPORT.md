## 2. Bade fark (sabse zaroori pehle)

### 2.1 Pahadon pe jangal (sabse bada fark, har drone shot mein)
- **Asli:** saari dhalaanein ghane, gehre hare jangal se poori dhaki hain (v07 poora, v08 0:22–0:38, v10 15:06). Zameen kahin nahi dikhti.
- **Game:** ped door-door hain aur beech mein **bhoori/beige zameen** dikhti hai (`ref_v08_lake_from_mallital`, `ref_v09_from_ropeway`). Door se pahad registaan jaisa lagta hai.
- **Theek karna:** dhalaan pe pedon ki ginti 3–4 guna, aur zameen ka texture gehra hara (ghaas/patte), bhoora nahi.

### 2.2 Jheel ka rang
- **Asli:** dhoop mein **firozi / hara-neela** (teal), kinaare pe aur halka hara (v08 0:18, 1:22, 2:14; v02 3:14).
- **Game:** gehra slate-neela (`ref_v08_lake_from_mallital`).
- **Theek karna:** paani ka rang teal ki taraf, kinaare ke paas halka.

### 2.3 Mallital shehar ki ghanatva aur chhatein
- **Asli:** Mallital ki dhalaan pe imaaratein **ek-doosre se sati hui**, upar tak bhari (v08 0:50–0:58). Chhatein **rangeen tin** ki: laal, hari, neeli, maroon. Deewarein safed/peeli/gulaabi.
- **Game:** imaaratein beech-beech mein khaali jagah ke saath, zyaadatar flat grey chhat wale dibbe (`ref_v08_mallital_town`, `ref_v09_town_below_ropeway`).
- **Theek karna:** Mallital mein ghar paas-paas, aur chhat dhalaan wali rangeen tin ki (flat nahi).

### 2.4 Mall Road ki imaaraton ka andaaz
- **Asli:** 2–4 manzil ke **angrezi daur ke bhawan**: dhalaan wali laal/hari tin ki chhat, lakdi ki balcony, safed-laal kinaari. Jaise Hotel India (v02 11:38, safed + laal), Hotel Gurdeep (v02 17:38, halka neela jaali wala), Boat House Club (v01 1:26, lambi neechi imaarat, laal tin chhat).
- **Game:** zyaadatar seedhe, flat chhat wale apartment jaise dibbe.
- **Theek karna:** Mall Road ki pehli line ki imaaraton ko dhalaan wali tin chhat + balcony wala style.

### 2.5 Ropeway ka upar wala sira aur khambe
- **Position:** OSM mein upar ka station (577, 505) pe hai. Game mein ropeway ~712 m lamba, upar ka sira Snow View plaza ke paas (~706, 582). **Upar wala station ~130 m zyaada poorv aur ~80 m zyaada uttar hai.**
- **Khambe:** asli mein **laal jaali wale (lattice) steel tower** (v09 0:38, 5:26). Game mein grey seedha khamba.
- **Theek karna:** upar ka station OSM wali jagah pe, khambe laal lattice.

### 2.6 Snow View ka upar wala hissa
- **Asli (v09 3:14–4:38):** railing wala gol view-deck, prayer flags ki dori, doorbeen, chhoti dukaanein, "Adventure Park" ka neela mehraab wala gate, dinosaur ke putle tin ki chhat ke neeche, deodar ka jangal.
- **Game (`ref_v09_snowview_platform`):** rangeen deewar wala lamba dibba aur peeche factory jaisi chimni wali imaarat. **Bilkul alag.**

### 2.7 Shaktipeeth gate ke paas police chowki
- **Asli (v01 0:26, 0:38):** gate ke bagal wali "Tourist Police" chowki **A-frame lakdi ka hut** hai: bahut tedhi, zameen tak aati bhoori chhat, saamne board.
- **Game:** laal-safed dibba + neela board.
- Gate aur chowki dono prefab mein hain. Aap jagah set karo, main chowki ka model A-frame bana deta hu.

- **Masjid:** jagah (-150, 52) sahi, muh Flats ki taraf sahi. **Oonchai galat:** game mein ~4 m oonche chabutre pe hai, asli mein road ke level pe (v08 1:42: gaadiyan masjid ke darwaze tak khadi hain). Scale theek (~30 m chaudi).
